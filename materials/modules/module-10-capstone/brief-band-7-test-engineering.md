## The capstone, from the spec

> Reusable fixtures or framework for an area, a pipeline with test selection, one performance or load test based on real clinical demand, and coaching two colleagues.

## Scope

An area of several products or services, and two colleagues.

Agree the exact scope with the product owner and mentor at hour 142.5, using the [scope agreement](capstone-scope-agreement.md), your Evidence 1 analysis, and your Evidence 9 estimate.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Reusable fixtures or a framework for the area: data builders, page objects, API helpers, and documentation, used by at least one other team's tests.
- [ ] A pipeline with test selection (and sharding if useful), and a quarantine policy.
- [ ] One performance or load test based on real clinical demand, with the demand model and results.
- [ ] A `spec/index.md` for the framework and tests.
- [ ] Traceability for any tests that protect hazards.
- [ ] A coaching log (Coaching others in automation) for two colleagues, with their feedback.
- [ ] A short engineering standards proposal for the area.

## Acceptance

The product owner and the Gate 4 panel will look for:

- The framework is used by at least one other person's tests.
- The pipeline is faster or more selective than before, with numbers.
- The performance test's demand model is agreed with someone who knows real clinical demand.
- Both coached colleagues' feedback is recorded.
- The product owner accepts the capstone.
- Synthetic data only, and no secrets in source control.
- You can explain every line you submitted.

## Support

Independent. The mentor is a lead test engineer or an external mentor.

## Presentation

20 minutes, plus questions, following the [presentation outline](presentation-outline.md). Include a live run: it is the Gate 4 Part D practical.

## Panel

Chair: the head of test, or a lead from outside your area. A developer from another team. The training lead. The clinical safety officer reviews traceability evidence in writing.

## Gate 4 thresholds

At least 90% in each of Parts A, B, and C of the capability self-assessment; Part D Meets; no skill more than one level below expected; test engineering at your automation target; capstone accepted by the product owner. See the spec for the full rules.
