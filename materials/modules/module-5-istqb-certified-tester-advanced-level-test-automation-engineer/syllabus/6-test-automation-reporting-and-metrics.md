# 6 Test Automation Reporting and Metrics

Part of the [CTAL-TAE syllabus, explained](index.md).

**Time in the syllabus:** 150 minutes. **Highest level:** K4. **Keywords to remember:** measurement, metric, test log, test progress report, test step.

## In plain words

What data to collect from the test automation and the system under test, how to analyse failures, and how to build and publish test progress reports for different stakeholders.
