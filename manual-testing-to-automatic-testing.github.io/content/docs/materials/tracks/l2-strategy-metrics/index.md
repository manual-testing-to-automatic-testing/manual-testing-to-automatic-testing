# L2 Automation strategy and metrics

**Tracks:** B6-QA, B7-TE (contribute), B7-TM (own). **Weeks:** 16 to 20.

**Outcomes:** contribute to (B6-QA, B7-TE) or own (B7-TM) the automation strategy for an area; define metrics for monitoring and controlling test activities.

**Evidence:** a strategy document and a metrics proposal, reviewed by the head of test. For B7-TM these feed the capstone.

- [Automation strategy template](automation-strategy-template.md)
- [Metrics proposal template](metrics-proposal-template.md)

## Activities

1. **Week 16:** read the testingexamples articles on the testing pyramid, CI, DevOps, and flow metrics again, with your area in mind. Collect the M1 automation candidate analyses from your area.
2. **Week 17:** draft the strategy with the template. Interview a developer, a product owner, and the clinical safety officer.
3. **Week 18:** draft the metrics proposal. Collect baseline numbers where you can, without new tools.
4. **Week 19:** review both drafts with your mentor and with the people you interviewed.
5. **Week 20:** submit to the head of test for review. B7-TM: carry both into the M10 capstone.

## Review criteria for the head of test

- The strategy is based on risk, including clinical risk, not on test counts.
- It pushes tests down the pyramid, and reserves browser tests for critical user journeys.
- It says what stays manual, and why.
- It covers test data, environments, CI, traceability, and ownership.
- The metrics measure flow and outcomes, not activity, and each one has an owner and a decision it supports.
- Both documents are written for a non-technical reader.
