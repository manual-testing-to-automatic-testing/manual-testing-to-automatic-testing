# Automation strategy and metrics

**Tracks:** Band 6 quality assurance, Band 7 test engineering (contribute), Band 7 test management (own). **Programme hours:** 112.5 to 150.

**Outcomes:** contribute to (Band 6 quality assurance, Band 7 test engineering) or own (Band 7 test management) the automation strategy for an area; define metrics for monitoring and controlling test activities.

**Evidence:** a strategy document and a metrics proposal, reviewed by the head of test. For Band 7 test management these feed the capstone.

- [Automation strategy template](automation-strategy-template.md)
- [Metrics proposal template](metrics-proposal-template.md)

## Activities

1. **Hours 172.5–180:** read the testingexamples articles on the testing pyramid, CI, DevOps, and flow metrics again, with your area in mind. Collect the Module 5 automation candidate analyses from your area.
2. **Hours 180–187.5:** draft the strategy with the template. Interview a developer, a product owner, and the clinical safety officer.
3. **Hours 187.5–195:** draft the metrics proposal. Collect baseline numbers where you can, without new tools.
4. **Hours 195–202.5:** review both drafts with your mentor and with the people you interviewed.
5. **Hours 202.5–210:** submit to the head of test for review. Band 7 test management: carry both into the Module 14 capstone.

## Review criteria for the head of test

- The strategy is based on risk, including clinical risk, not on test counts.
- It pushes tests down the pyramid, and reserves browser tests for critical user journeys.
- It says what stays manual, and why.
- It covers test data, environments, CI, traceability, and ownership.
- The metrics measure flow and outcomes, not activity, and each one has an owner and a decision it supports.
- Both documents are written for a non-technical reader.
