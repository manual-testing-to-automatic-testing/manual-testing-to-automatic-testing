## How to read each module

Each module page has the same sections:

- **Purpose:** why the module exists.
- **Outcomes:** the learning outcomes (learning outcome ids) from the spec.
- **Depth by track:** copied from the spec: Read and discuss, With support (apply with support), Independent (apply independently), or Lead or coach (apply and lead or coach others). A dash means the track does not take the module.
- **Session plan:** sessions, durations, and format. **Core** sessions are shared by the whole cohort. **Breakout** sessions are for named tracks.
- **Activities.**
- **Evidence:** the evidence id (Evidence 1 to 17), with the variants for each track.
- **Assessment:** which gate reviews the evidence.
- **Resources.**

## Time

The programme is **320 hours** of protected learning time for every track: 60.5 hours for the basics, Modules 1 to 3; 28 hours for the two ISTQB certifications, Modules 4 and 5; 191 hours for Modules 6 to 16, the role and health care foundations, and Gates 0 to 4; then 40 hours for Module 17, the Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, and a 0.5-hour buffer before its exam. Tracks differ in depth, evidence, and capstone, not in hours. The default pace is 7.5 hours a week, 20% of a 37.5-hour working week, so the programme runs over about 43 calendar weeks; the learning agreement may set another pace. Band 3 and Band 4 may extend to 380 hours; their gates then fall at hours 88.5, 152.5, 213.5, 269.5, and 340, and Module 17 runs in hours 340–380.

The timeline is in programme hours, counted from 0. The table below splits the 320 hours into blocks of 7.5 hours (a week at the default pace); most blocks add up to 7.5 hours; a block where a module ends can hold a little more, and the last block holds the Green Belt exam and a 0.5-hour buffer.5-hour block that ends Module 5 with its exam, and the last, which holds the 2.5-hour Green Belt exam. Besides the core modules, the blocks hold:

- **Role foundations:** 1 hour in every 7.5 hours of learning from hour 97.5 (the individual learning plan meeting in hours 88.5–97.5 starts it): 23 hours.
- **Health care foundations:** 1 hour in each block from hour 97.5 to hour 152.5: 7 hours.
- **Gates 1 to 4:** 2.5 hours each, at hours 136.5, 183, 221.5, and 279.5, for the self-assessment, calibration, and the Part D practical: 10 hours. Gate 0 is part of Module 6.
- **Real automation on the team's product:** 1.5 hours in each block from hour 160.5 to hour 221.5: 12 hours. From hour 221.5, Module 14 and the capstone use the team's product directly.

Track modules for Band 6 and Band 7 (Coaching others in automation from hour 160.5; Acceptance test automation for Band 6 quality assurance from hour 183; Automation strategy and metrics, Frameworks and non-functional testing, and Leading teams through automation adoption from hour 206) take part of those tracks' breakout and real automation time. Band 3 does not take Module 12, so Band 3's Module 12 hours go to Role foundations and real automation at Band 3 depth.

Gate 5 is a follow-up about six months after Gate 4, outside the 320 hours. Module 17 has no gate of its own: the certification body's exam and the project review assess it, and a person completes the programme with Gate 4 and Evidence 17.

Other people's time, per participant: the mentor gives a 1-hour start, a 1-hour check-in, and a 1-hour walkthrough in each basics module in hours 0–60.5 (about 7 hours), 1.5 hours per 7.5 learning hours for hours 88.5–136.5, then 1 hour per 7.5 learning hours, with 1-hour capstone reviews (about 36.5 hours); the line manager 1 hour per 15 learning hours plus 2 hours per gate (about 28 hours); developers about 1 hour per 7.5 learning hours; the training lead 1 hour per 7.5 cohort learning hours in hours 0–60.5, then 3.75 hours per 7.5 cohort learning hours, for a cohort of up to 12 (98 hours).
